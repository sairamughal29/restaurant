Since 2016

Cooked With Passion, Served With Pride

What started as a small family kitchen on Food Street has grown into the city's favourite spot for premium fast food. We believed then, as we do now, that great food comes from three things: fresh ingredients, fire-tested recipes, and people who genuinely care.

Every burger patty is hand-pressed, every pizza dough is stretched to order, and every sauce is made in-house. No shortcuts, no compromises — just honest cooking with bold flavours.
